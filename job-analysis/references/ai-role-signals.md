# AI Role Signals

Use this reference only when the JD suggests AI products, AI features, model-enabled workflows, intelligent tools, or human-AI interaction.

## Common AI product expectations

Distinguish among these patterns:

### AI product strategy
- Identifying where AI creates user or business value
- Prioritizing use cases, feasibility, and trust tradeoffs
- Connecting model capability to product opportunity

### AI feature or workflow design
- Designing user flows with generation, recommendation, or automation
- Handling confidence, review, fallbacks, and error states
- Considering human-in-the-loop interaction and explainability

### AI application delivery
- Working with engineers on model integration, evaluation, iteration, and shipping
- Understanding prompt design, retrieval, workflows, or quality evaluation at a practical level

## Signals that strengthen AI-role fit

- Shipped AI-related features, experiments, or internal tools
- Framed AI use cases around user problems rather than trend language
- Considered quality, trust, latency, hallucination, or review loops
- Worked on automation, copilots, recommendations, search, knowledge tools, or intelligent workflows
- Learned quickly in ambiguous technical spaces

## Resume rewrite guidance for AI roles

Prefer these angles:
- Problem solved with AI or automation
- Workflow or user experience change enabled by AI
- Collaboration with engineering or data on feasibility and iteration
- Practical evaluation logic such as quality criteria, feedback loops, or adoption metrics

Avoid weak claims like:
- "Passionate about AI"
- "Closely follow AI trends"
- "Interested in large models"

These only help if paired with shipped work, experiments, research, or concrete projects.

## Hidden screening questions

When reviewing an AI-related profile, recruiters or hiring managers may ask:
- Has this candidate actually shipped or tested AI-enabled work?
- Can this person translate model capability into product decisions?
- Does this person understand trust, quality, and UX constraints of AI features?
- Is the candidate hands-on enough to work with technical teams without pretending to be an ML engineer?
